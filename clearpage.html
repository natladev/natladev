What it does

On iPhone, iPhone Duo and iPad

One app, laid out for each screen. On iPhone Duo it follows the fold: closed, half-open like a laptop, or fully open.

Math is a reading problem too

Dyscalculia gets almost nothing. Every major reader assumes the problem is words, so notation is left exactly as it was: symbols that look alike, meanings you're expected to already know, formulas no voice reads correctly. ClearPage treats math as part of the page.

  • 35 symbols, color-anchored ∈ against ⊂, ≤ against ≥, ℝ against R. Look-alikes get consistent colors so they stop swapping places.
  • Every symbol explained Tap any one for a plain-language meaning card. No prior notation knowledge assumed.
  • Formulas read aloud, correctly Spoken as math, not as a string of characters, so a formula can be heard as well as seen.

New in 2.0: two-dimensional math. Fractions, sums, integrals and derivatives are read as structure instead of being flattened into a line of characters. Select one on the page and go through it piece by piece.

New in 3.0

Summarize and simplify any page

Some pages are simply too much: too long, or written in words nobody uses. ClearPage can now give you the short version, or the same page in plainer words. Both are written on your device with Apple Intelligence, so the page never leaves your phone.

Good to know: both appear in your own typeface, spacing and colors, and can be shared or saved. They need an iPhone or iPad with Apple Intelligence; on other devices ClearPage works exactly as before. A summary or a rewrite can miss or change a detail, so the original page is always one tap away.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes, no ads, no subscription, nothing to unlock. Accessibility shouldn't come at a cost. You can chip in on the support page if you'd like.

Does it work offline?

Completely. OCR and Read Aloud run on-device, no Wi-Fi, no signal needed.

What about my privacy?

Your photos and profile never leave your device. No accounts, no network, no analytics. Privacy policy.

Which languages?

OCR and the full interface in English, Spanish, German, and Simplified Chinese. ClearPage follows your device language.

What do I need?

An iPhone or iPad. Grab it free on the App Store and start scanning right away.

Is it accessible?

Localized in EN, ES, DE & ZH, scales with Dynamic Type for low vision, and fully VoiceOver-labeled with Reduce Motion support.

Free on iPhone and iPad

No account, no ads, no Wi-Fi needed.
